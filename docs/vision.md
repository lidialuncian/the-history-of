# Product vision — The History Of...

## Concept
An immersive, interactive history platform for exploring the origins and evolution of subjects such as ballet, opera, and tango.

## Experience
Each subject should feel like entering its own world, not reading a generic encyclopedia page. Combine long-form editorial storytelling with images, maps, timelines, video, historical figures, quotations, surprising facts, cross-subject connections, and depictions in film, theater, and music.

## Visual direction
The platform homepage acts as a welcoming digital museum and gateway to different histories. Subject pages share a coherent UX foundation but express distinct personalities:
- Ballet: romantic, graceful, atmospheric.
- Tango: fearless, bold, passionate, romantic.
- Opera: a distinct direction to explore later.

## Audience and product principles
- Curious readers should be able to browse without specialist knowledge.
- Prioritize editorial quality, clear chronology, context, and delight.
- Make rich interactions optional and accessible; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Attribute media and historical claims appropriately.
- Design mobile and desktop experiences intentionally.

## Initial scope
1. Homepage and discovery.
2. Ballet as the first immersive subject page.
3. Shared subject-theme architecture that can later support tango and opera.

## Open questions
Audience positioning, content sourcing/editorial workflow, monetization, search, localization, and authentication are not decided.
